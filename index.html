Getting there

Leave the
car at home

There are no cars in the festival fields. Come by train, shuttle or coach, and book everything in the app.

Train10 min

Shuttle trains every 10 minutes from Manchester Piccadilly and Sheffield to Hope and Bamford stations, then a short walk to the gates.

Uber shuttlesFree

Uber shuttle buses run from both stations and the Hathersage park and ride. Electric Uber Green rides wait at the coach park.

Coach20+ cities

Official coaches from cities across the UK, including London, Birmingham, Leeds, Liverpool and Newcastle.

CarP&R only

Drivers use the Hathersage park and ride. Pre-book your parking pass in the app.

Good to know

FAQ

Who can come?

BeatWave is 18+. Bring photo ID to the gate, as everyone is checked.

Will my phone work in the Peak District?

Mobile signal in the Hope Valley is patchy, so free Starlink Wi-Fi covers the whole arena and every campsite. The app also works offline for set times and maps.

Can I still pay in instalments?

Klarna Pay in 6 closed on 28 February so that every plan is paid off before the festival. Tickets bought now are paid in full.

What happens if an artist cancels?

We'll tell you in the app as soon as we know and confirm a replacement act. Your ticket stays valid. Check the ticket terms for refund details.

Is the festival accessible?

Yes. There are accessible viewing platforms, an accessible campsite with flushing toilets, accessible shuttle buses from both stations and a free carer ticket scheme.

Can I bring food and drink?

You can bring food and sealed soft drinks into the campsites. The arena has the Local Heroes food zone and free water refill points.
